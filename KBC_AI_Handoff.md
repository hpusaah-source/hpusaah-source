## 1. Bottom line

KBC spent **~$676M of mostly borrowed money** buying out the land rights on the Trang Cat
project. It now has to carry that cost until the land earns revenue in 2027.

The negative cash flow, the doubled inventory, the tripled debt, the weak Q1 — all
downstream of that one decision. [O]

**The asset purchase was smart. The funding structure is the risk.** [O]
**Solvent, but liquidity-strained. Overall 6/10.** [O]

## 3. What v1.0 got wrong

**3.1 The cash flow was a land purchase, not a collection failure.**
v1.0 read −$685M as receivables going bad. It wasn't. KBC paid **100% of the Trang Cat
land-use fee — ~$676M — for all 2.5 million m² of long-tenure residential land, ahead of
deadline.** [V] That is one-off, discretionary, **finished**, and converts cash into the
most legally secure form of land title in Vietnam. Far better than v1.0 assumed. [O]

**3.2 Financing risk now outranks approvals risk.**
Debt went **<$190M (mid-2024) → ~$1.07bn (end-2025) → $1.14bn (Q1/2026)** — ~6x in under
two years [D], and now **exceeds equity**. Management confirmed at the 2026 AGM that bank
liquidity tightened, property credit tightened, rates rose, and disbursement got hard. [V]

**3.3 Trang Due 3 has cleared approvals.** The **30 ha LG contract is signed** [V]. Watch
handover and revenue recognition now, not permits. [O]

**3.4 Stock direction claim conflicts with data. [?]**
v1.0 said −10% June→July. Data says it **bottomed ~28.x in early June and broke out ~11
June** [V]. Live quotes conflicted ($1.06–$1.40); confirm on a broker terminal.
52-week range ~$0.76–$1.64 [V]. Targets: BSC $1.61, TPS $1.63, KBSV $1.34 [V].

## 4. Where the cash actually comes from

Income is *not* funding this, and was never meant to. FY2025 revenue of $254M on a $2.66bn
asset base is ~10% asset turnover [D]. Q1/2026 revenue was $51M against $1.14bn of debt.

**The four real sources:** [V]

| Source | Approx. size | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| **New long-term borrowing** | **~+$910M** since mid-2024 | The dominant source [D] |
| Private share placement | ~$227M | 250M shares @ VND 23,900 [V] |
| Partner deposit | $215M | Refundable — not KBC's money [V] |
| Operating revenue | $254M (FY25) | Smallest contributor [V] |

**Placement proceeds went to debt, not growth:** $168M to Saigon–Bac Giang IP,
$56M to Saigon–Hai Phong IP, $4M VietinBank, $3.6M Saigon Tay Bac. [V]

### Three things that make this work — and one that should worry you

**(a) The debt is mostly long-term.** Of ~$1.07bn, **over $912M is long-term**, with only
**~$38M of bonds (10.5% coupon) maturing in 2026.** [V] There is no near-term maturity
wall. This is the single biggest reason this is a strain, not a crisis. [O]

**(b) Much of the interest isn't paid from profit — it's capitalised.** A developer can add
interest on assets under construction to the project's cost instead of expensing it. So
part of the ~$40M/yr interest bill [D] never hits the P&L; it gets parked in inventory.
[I] That's how a company earning $8.9M a quarter carries $1.14bn of debt without visible
collapse. It also means **reported interest understates the real burden, and inventory
keeps inflating with accrued financing cost.** [I] Legal and normal — but it only works if
the land eventually sells above cost-plus-accumulated-interest. [O]

**(c) Remaining spend is phaseable.** The land fee had a hard legal deadline. The
2026–2028 infrastructure build does not. KBC can throttle its own burn if funding
tightens. [I]

**(d) The cash balance is thinner than it looks.** Reported cash and deposits of ~$263M
included **~$215M that was a refundable partner deposit** for Trang Cat brokerage —
leaving roughly **$48M of genuinely own cash.** [V][D] Treat headline cash with caution.

**Contradiction to watch:** the 2026 AGM approved a **20% cash dividend (~$77M)** [V][E]
while simultaneously seeking new capital and restructuring debt. Paying out cash you are
concurrently raising is difficult to defend on capital-allocation grounds. [O]

## 9. Scenarios [O]

**Base ~50%** — some handovers land, equity raised at mediocre price (10–15% dilution),
FY26 hits 60–75% of target. Debt stabilises. Stock range-bound, mild upward bias.

**Bull ~25%** — Que Vo and Trang Due 3 complete on time, equity raised into strength,
Trang Cat wholesale validates pricing. Re-rate toward $1.60+ targets.

**Bear ~25%** — a seventh straight miss, credit stays tight, forced issuance at a
depressed price. Interest consumes operating profit. Retest ~$0.80.

Genuinely two-sided. Not a low-variance holding — do not size it like one. [O]

## 12. Standing instructions

1. Label every claim: Verified / Derived / Inferred / Estimate / Opinion / Unverified.
2. **Open with cash flow, not profit.** Profit is the least informative line here.
3. Always separate *what was bought* from *how it was funded*. The first is fine; the
   second is the risk.
4. Strip refundable deposits and partner money out of headline cash.
5. Check how much interest is capitalised — the P&L understates the burden.
6. Never treat a company target as a base case. Six misses is the prior.
7. Ignore news not touching cash, debt, approvals, handovers, or valuation.
8. Confirm live prices on a broker terminal; web sources cache stale quotes.
