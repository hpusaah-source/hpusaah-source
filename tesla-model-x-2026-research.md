# Tesla Model X — 2026 Research Report

**Market:** United States · **Compiled:** July 17, 2026 · **Currency:** USD

> ⚠️ **Prices and options change often.** Tesla updates configurator pricing without notice.
> Always confirm the live figures at **[tesla.com/modelx](https://www.tesla.com/modelx)** before purchasing.

---

## 🚨 Most important fact first: 2026 is the *final* model year

Tesla has confirmed it is **ending production of the Model X (and Model S)**. Elon Musk announced
on the Q4 2025 earnings call (late January 2026) that both premium models would wind down in
**Q2 2026**, with the Fremont, California line repurposed toward new initiatives (including the
Optimus humanoid robot). The final Model S reportedly rolled off the line in **May 2026**, and the
Model X followed on a similar timeline.

**What this means for a buyer today:**
- The **2026 Model X is both the newest and the last** version Tesla will build.
- New units may still be **orderable from remaining inventory** while supplies last, but this is a
  discontinued, end-of-run vehicle — not an ongoing model.
- Tesla's remaining lineup continues with the Model 3, Model Y, and Cybertruck.

**$99,990 / $114,990**. The $99,990 / $114,990 figures are the current all-in starting prices and
already bundle Full Self-Driving (Supervised) and lifetime free Supercharging.

## 6. Options & packages

- **Luxe Package (bundled into 2026 price):** the headline change for the final run. Reported to
  include **Full Self-Driving (Supervised)**, **lifetime free Supercharging**, **four years of
  premium service**, and **lifetime Premium Connectivity**. This is the main reason 2026 prices
  jumped ~$10k over 2025.
- **Full Self-Driving (Supervised):** included via the Luxe Package on the 2026 Model X. Purchased
  standalone on other vehicles it has historically run ~$8,000; Tesla also offers it as a
  **subscription at $99/month** (lowered from $199 earlier in 2026).
- **Six-seat interior:** premium second-row captain's-chairs layout — historically about **$6,500**
  when optioned (confirm current price on the configurator).
- **Tow package:** enables the up-to-**5,000 lb** towing capacity.
- **Steering wheel:** round wheel or yoke, buyer's choice at no extra cost.

---

## 7. Charging summary

- **Native NACS port** — plugs directly into Tesla Superchargers with **no adapter**.
- **Up to 250 kW** peak charging → roughly **179 miles added in 15 minutes**.
- **Lifetime free Supercharging** is included via the Luxe Package on the 2026 cars — a notable perk
  Tesla removed years ago and brought back only for this final run.
- A CCS/J1772 adapter (sold separately) is needed for most non-Tesla public chargers; the portable
  **Mobile Connector** for home/240V charging is typically **not included** and sold separately.

---

## 8. What's new / notable for 2026

- **Final model year** — production ending Q2 2026 (see top of report).
- **Luxe Package** bundling FSD (Supervised) + lifetime free Supercharging + premium service/connectivity.
- **Frost Blue Metallic** new exterior color; limited **Lunar Silver** (now sold out).
- New **22″ aero wheel designs**.
- Carries over the spring-2025 refresh: **front bumper camera**, updated **ambient lighting**,
  quieter cabin, revised suspension/ride, and slightly improved range.

---

## 9. Bottom line

The **2026 Tesla Model X** is a 5-to-7-seat luxury electric SUV with falcon-wing doors, offered in
two trims — the **$99,990 dual-motor All-Wheel Drive** (670 hp, 352 mi, 0–60 in 3.8 s) and the
**$114,990 tri-motor Plaid** (1,020 hp, 335 mi, 0–60 in 2.5 s) — before ~$1,640 in destination/order
fees. Both now ship with the **Luxe Package** (FSD Supervised + lifetime free Supercharging).
It offers 7 exterior colors (only Stealth Grey free), 3 interior themes, 20″ or 22″ wheels, and
optional 6-/7-seat layouts, tows up to 5,000 lb, and Superchargers at up to 250 kW over a native
NACS port. The headline caveat: **this is the last Model X Tesla will build** — 2026 closes out the
model's ~10-year run.
